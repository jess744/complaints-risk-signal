RAG Framework
Complaint categories: Green <10% above prior month. Amber 10–25%. Red >25%. Comparison: current month projected vs prior month actual. September is 6 of 30 days complete (20%), so all complaint figures are MTD ÷ 6 × 30 projections — the widest projection basis of any report in this series, and the least reliable. Prior-month comparison uses August as restated on this week's refresh (Escalations 145, Service Errors 22, Dispute Risk 55, Policy & Product 72).
Reputational: Green when public scores and weekly review quality are at or above target (AU ≥4.8, US ≥4.7). Public score — Red if 0.2 below target (AU <4.6, US <4.5). Weekly review quality — Amber if a week falls below target; Red if 2 consecutive weeks 0.2 below target. This week the weekly series did not refresh, so the Amber is carried on the 9 and 16 Aug US points.
Overall: Green = all Green. Amber = any Amber or 1 Red. Red = 2+ Reds or 3+ Ambers. This week: 1 Red + 2 Amber = Amber. Note the framework is one Amber away from Red on categories that all flip band on a single event — so a Red next week would carry no more information than this Amber does. Read the composition, not the banner.
