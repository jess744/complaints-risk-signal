RAG Framework
Complaint categories: Green <10% above prior month. Amber 10–25%. Red >25%. Comparison: current month (projected from MTD ÷ days elapsed × days in month) vs prior month actual. August projection based on 23 completed days (×1.35 factor) — three-quarters of the month banked, so projections are now stable and the Service Errors Red also holds on raw actuals without any projection.
Reputational: Green when public scores and weekly review quality are at or above target (AU ≥4.8, US ≥4.7). Public score — Red if 0.2 below target (AU <4.6, US <4.5). Weekly review quality — Amber if a week falls below target; Red if 2 consecutive weeks 0.2 below target.
Overall: Green = all Green. Amber = any Amber or 1 Red. Red = 2+ Reds or 3+ Ambers. This week: 1 Red + 1 Amber = Amber. Note the framework treats one Red the same as one Amber — if the Service Errors trend continues into September the Overall rating will understate it, so read the composition, not just the banner.
