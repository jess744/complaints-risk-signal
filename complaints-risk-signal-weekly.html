ℹ The assumption that would flip this conclusion: within-month backfill. If tagging lags by a few days, last week's day 8–13 counts were understated and have since filled in — which would inflate the apparent days 14–20 increment and overstate the acceleration. Two things argue against it. First, the August base is unchanged on this refresh (145 / 22 / 55 / 72, identical to last week), so the series is not being retrospectively restated at the month level. Second, backfill would lift all four series, and Service Errors went the other way (0.57/day in days 14–20 vs 0.69/day in days 1–13). It is still possible that recent-day lag is partly responsible, and it cannot be ruled out from the chart alone. What would settle it: re-read the same Monthly charts next Monday — if days 14–20 restate upward again, the series has a lag and the weekly increments are unreliable; if they hold at 49 / 4 / 16 / 29, the acceleration is real. Second caveat — sample: at n=29 for the P&P increment, Poisson SD is ±5.4, so the jump from 2.62 to 4.14/day is roughly 2 SD on its own. It is the corroboration across three independent series in the same week that makes it worth acting on, not any one of them.
